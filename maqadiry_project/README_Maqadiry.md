# Maqadiry

**Maqadiry** is an e-commerce MVP developed for the IT473 E-Commerce Systems course.  
The project presents a home-cooking support platform that provides fresh, pre-cut, and portioned meal ingredients based on the selected meal and number of people.

## Project Idea

Maqadiry helps customers cook at home with less planning and preparation.  
Instead of buying bulk ingredients from grocery stores or ordering ready-made meals from food delivery apps, users can choose a meal and receive the ingredients prepared and portioned for cooking.
The platform does **not** sell cooked meals.  
It provides ready-to-cook ingredient sets that support the home-cooking experience.

## Main Problem

Home cooking usually requires several steps before cooking starts:

- Choosing what to cook
- Estimating the right ingredient quantities
- Buying ingredients from stores
- Washing, cutting, and preparing ingredients
- Understanding basic nutrition information

These steps can be time-consuming and may lead to over-purchasing, food waste, and confusion, especially for students, beginner cooks, small families, and busy employees.

## Solution

Maqadiry provides a simple e-commerce experience where users can:

- Browse available meal ingredient options
- View meal details before ordering
- See price, basic ingredients, allergens, and nutrition information
- Add selected meals to the cart
- Adjust the number of people
- Complete a simulated checkout flow
- Receive post-order recipe and quantity guidance through the MVP confirmation flow

## Project Goal

The goal of Maqadiry is to make home cooking easier, clearer, and more organized while preserving the cooking experience itself.

## What Maqadiry Provides

- Fresh, pre-cut ingredients
- Measured portions based on the number of people
- Nutrition information
- Recipe guidance

## Target Customers

Maqadiry targets customers who want to cook at home but need support with planning, preparation, or portion control, including:

- Students
- Beginner cooks
- Busy employees
- Small families
- Health-conscious customers
- Individuals living alone

## Business Model

Maqadiry uses a direct sales model.  
Revenue is generated by selling meal ingredient sets priced per serving.

**Pricing Formula:**

```text
Total Price = Price per Serving × Number of People + Delivery Fee
```

A fixed delivery fee is applied per order.
